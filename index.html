About

Chemical engineer with a passion for green processes

I am a Chemical Engineer (B.Sc., COMSATS University Islamabad) currently completing my Master's in Advanced Green Engineering & Sustainable Management at FH Westsächsische Hochschule Zwickau, Germany.

My research interests center on CO₂ capture materials, supercritical fluid processes, and circular economy applications. At thyssenkrupp Calvion, I supported pilot-scale Direct Air Capture testing. At the University of Cádiz as an Erasmus+ intern, I developed nano-CaO sorbents from eggshell waste — a first-of-its-kind processing chain.

I combine strong analytical laboratory skills (XRD, BET, FTIR, TGA, SEM) with programming in Python and process simulation in Aspen HYSYS. I hold a NEBOSH IGC certification in occupational health and safety.
